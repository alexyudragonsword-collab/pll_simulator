1Design philosophy设计思路

1.1 One contract, two domains1.1 统一接口，双域建模

Every architecture implements the same contract: analyze() returns a linear phase-domain model (loop metrics, per-source phase-noise breakdown, analytic spurs, integrated jitter) and simulate() runs a nonlinear behavioral time-domain model (lock acquisition, cycle slips, quantization patterns, calibration convergence). The two are not independent artifacts — every dual-domain architecture carries a regression test forcing the settled time-domain periodogram to match the linear model within 2–3 dB band-averaged. When the two disagree, one of them is wrong, and finding out which one taught us most of the lessons in §9. 每种架构实现同一接口：analyze() 返回线性相位域模型（环路指标、分源相噪分解、解析杂散、积分 jitter），simulate() 运行非线性行为级时域模型（锁定捕获、周期滑移、量化图样、校准收敛）。两者不是各自独立的产物——每个双域架构都带一条回归测试，强制时域稳态周期图与线性模型在带平均意义下 2–3 dB 内吻合。两者不一致时必有一方是错的，而查明是哪一方错了，正是第 9 节大部分经验教训的来源。

1.2 Grid-evaluated frequency responses, not polynomial transfer functions1.2 网格求值频响，而非多项式传函

A 12 GHz carrier with a 100 kHz-class loop bandwidth puts ~105 of dynamic range into polynomial coefficients and makes scipy.signal.TransferFunction numerically fragile. Instead, every response is a complex array evaluated on a log frequency grid (core/freqresp.py), with operator algebra (+ − × ÷, feedback()). s-domain integrators, z-domain accumulators, pure delays e−sT and ZOH sinc responses mix freely in one expression — which is exactly what sampled PLL loops need. 12 GHz 载波配 100 kHz 量级环路带宽，多项式系数动态范围高达 ~105，scipy.signal.TransferFunction 在数值上不可靠。因此所有频响都是在对数频率网格上求值的复数数组（core/freqresp.py），支持运算符代数（+ − × ÷、feedback()）。s 域积分器、z 域累加器、纯延迟 e−sT、ZOH sinc 可以在同一表达式中自由混合——这正是采样型 PLL 环路所需要的。

1.3 One PSD convention, locked by tests1.3 全库统一 PSD 约定，测试锁定

Sφ(f) [rad²/Hz, DSB]  ↔  L(f) = Sφ(f)/2 [dBc/Hz]   |   σt = √(∫Sφdf) / (2πfout)

Internal PSDs are double-sideband Sφ in rad²/Hz; plots and spot numbers are L(f) in dBc/Hz. A unit test pins the convention (a flat −100 dBc/Hz profile must produce the textbook jitter number), eliminating the classic factor-of-2 bug family at the root. 内部 PSD 为双边带 Sφ（rad²/Hz），绘图与点值用 L(f)（dBc/Hz）。单元测试锁定该约定（平坦 −100 dBc/Hz 曲线必须积出教科书 jitter 数值），从根上消灭经典的 2 倍系数错误。

1.4 Reference-edge-driven time domain — no nanosecond time stepping1.4 参考沿驱动的时域引擎——不做纳秒级步进

Simulating a 12 GHz output with brute-force time steps is hopeless in Python. The engine advances one step per reference cycle: divider/counter edges come from closed-form phase accumulation; the passive loop filter uses exact discrete state-space updates (precomputed eAT, verified against solve_ivp to 10−6); oscillator noise is injected as per-cycle phase increments from a Leeson-profile generator (random-walk white FM + FFT-shaped flicker FM + additive floor, PSD-verified to ±1 dB). 400k reference cycles simulate in tens of seconds. 用暴力时间步进在 Python 里仿 12 GHz 输出毫无希望。引擎每参考周期推进一步：分频器/计数器沿由闭式相位累加求出；无源环路滤波器用精确离散状态空间更新（预计算 eAT，对 solve_ivp 验证到 10−6）；振荡器噪声由 Leeson 模型发生器按周期注入相位增量（随机游走白频调噪声 + FFT 整形闪烁频调噪声 + 加性噪底，PSD 验证 ±1 dB）。40 万参考周期数十秒仿完。

1.5 Spur analysis: analytic where sampling hides it, FFT where it shows1.5 杂散分析：采样看不见的用解析式，看得见的用 FFT

A reference-rate simulation aliases any spur at ≥ fref/2 to DC — so reference spurs are estimated analytically (ripple charge → control-line first harmonic → narrowband-FM sideband). Fractional spurs (< fref/2) are measured from the full-length periodogram with local-noise-floor subtraction. The ILCM additionally records the intra-period phase ramp analytically at M× oversampling so the fref-offset injection spur becomes visible in the FFT — and it lands within 1 dB of the sawtooth-ripple analytic formula (§6). 参考速率采样会把 ≥ fref/2 处的杂散混叠到直流——因此参考杂散用解析式估计（纹波电荷 → 控制线一次谐波 → 窄带调频边带）。小数杂散（< fref/2）从全长周期图测量并扣除局部噪底。ILCM 额外以 M 倍过采样解析记录周期内相位斜坡，使 fref 偏移处的注入杂散在 FFT 中可见——它与锯齿纹波解析式吻合在 1 dB 内（见第 6 节）。

3Sub-sampling PLL + FLL亚采样 PLL + FLL

3.1 Why it wins — stated precisely3.1 优势所在——精确表述

The reference edge samples the VCO waveform directly: vs = A·sin(φerr), so the PD gain A [V/rad] is referred to output phase. Charge-pump and loop-filter noise are therefore not multiplied by N — that is the entire advantage. Reference noise still multiplies by N (inherent to ×N frequency multiplication; no architecture escapes it). With the same reference and VCO class as ex01, in-band drops enough for ~150 fs. 参考沿直接采样 VCO 波形：vs = A·sin(φerr)，鉴相增益 A [V/rad] 以输出相位为基准。因此电荷泵与环路滤波器噪声不被 N 倍增——这就是全部优势所在。参考噪声仍然 ×N（×N 倍频固有属性，任何架构都躲不掉）。与 ex01 相同的参考和 VCO 等级下，带内噪声下降到约 150 fs。

Gol = A·gm·G·Z·2πKvco/s  (no 1/N)  |   implemented exactly in z:z 域精确实现： Gol(z) = Kq·Hq(z)·2πKvcoT·z−1/(1−z−1)

Because SSPLLs run aggressive UGB/fref ratios (1/16 here), analyze() uses the exact discrete loop: Hq(z) = C(I−Adz−1)−1AdB is derived from the same state-space matrices the simulator steps — model and simulation share one update law by construction. Loop-injected noise images beyond fref/2 are ZOH-attenuated; the continuous VCO keeps E→1 there (the sampled model's image dips are not physical for it). 由于 SSPLL 运行激进的 UGB/fref 比（此处 1/16），analyze() 使用精确离散环路：Hq(z) = C(I−Adz−1)−1AdB 由仿真器逐拍使用的同一组状态空间矩阵导出——模型与仿真在构造上共享同一更新律。环内注入噪声在 fref/2 以上的镜像按 ZOH 衰减；连续 VCO 在该区域保持 E→1（采样模型的镜像凹陷对它不物理）。

4Reference-sampling PLL参考采样 PLL

The exact dual of §3: a divided VCO edge samples the reference sine, so PD gain is referred to reference phase and the identical sampler front-end noise now multiplies by N to the output. Wider capture (±π at the reference carrier), a conventional divider for acquisition — but ~20logN worse in-band PD noise. The two chapters together are the honest architecture comparison. 第 3 节的严格对偶：分频后的 VCO 沿采样参考正弦，鉴相增益以参考相位为基准，同样的采样器前端噪声此时 ×N 到输出。捕获范围更宽（参考载波上 ±π），且有常规分频器辅助捕获——代价是带内 PD 噪声差约 20logN。两章合起来即是诚实的架构对比。

6Injection-locked clock multiplier + FTL注入锁定时钟倍频器 + FTL

6.1 The realignment recursion6.1 重排递推模型

e[n+1] = (1−β)·wrap±π(e[n] + Δφdrift + φosc[n]) + β·φinj[n]

Exact for pulse injection at reference rate. Oscillator noise enters as per-cycle phase increments, giving the phase-PSD NTF (1−β)(1−z−1)/(1−(1−β)z−1) — a highpass with injection bandwidth ≈ βfref/2π (36 MHz at β=0.6, fref=250 MHz); the reference path is the complementary lowpass ×N. The ±π wrap is essential: injection realigns toward the nearest edge, which is what limits the lock range to βfref/2 (75 MHz here; a linear recursion never slips — see §9). The Adler LC-tank bound f0Iinj/(2QIosc) is reported alongside as a cross-check. 对参考速率的脉冲注入是精确的。振荡器噪声以每周期相位增量进入，相位 PSD 的 NTF 为 (1−β)(1−z−1)/(1−(1−β)z−1)——高通，注入带宽 ≈ βfref/2π（β=0.6、fref=250 MHz 时为 36 MHz）；参考路径为互补低通 ×N。±π 卷绕不可省略：注入只能向最近的沿重排，这正是锁定范围被限制在 βfref/2（此处 75 MHz）的原因——线性递推永远不会失锁（见第 9 节）。Adler LC 谐振腔界 f0Iinj/(2QIosc) 一并给出作为交叉校验。

9Caveats and lessons learned注意事项与经验教训

Model validity boundaries (also in the README): 模型有效边界（README 中亦有记载）：
  • CPPLL/SPLL analyze() is continuous-time (warned when UGB > fref/10); SSPLL and ADPLL use exact discrete models. CPPLL/SPLL 的 analyze() 为连续时间近似（UGB > fref/10 时告警）；SSPLL 与 ADPLL 用精确离散模型。
  • Reference-rate simulation cannot show spurs at ≥ fref/2 — reported analytically instead. 参考速率仿真无法显示 ≥ fref/2 的杂散——改用解析式报告。
  • TDC/DTC quantization is white in the linear model; its deterministic (tonal) component appears only in the time domain. TDC/DTC 量化在线性模型中按白噪声处理；其确定性（音调性）成分只出现在时域。
  • BBPD linearization is ±3 dB-class in-band; the time domain is the reference for BB loops. BBPD 线性化带内精度约 ±3 dB；BB 环路以时域为准。

The following six issues were exposed by simulation, not anticipated on paper. Each is now encoded in the library and its tests. 以下六个问题都是仿真暴露的，纸面设计阶段未能预见。每一条现已固化在库代码与测试中。

9.1 Divider-edge timing is not output phase9.1 分频器沿时序不是输出相位

In a fractional-N loop the divider edge wobbles by the full DSM residue (~1 UI ≈ 166 ps at 6 GHz), but the loop lowpasses that wobble — the VCO output never follows it. Recording divider-edge timing as "output phase" inflated jitter by 600×. The fix: accumulate true output phase as ∫(fv−fout)dt + oscillator noise. 小数N环路中分频器沿抖动幅度等于完整 DSM 残差（~1 UI，6 GHz 下约 166 ps），但环路会低通滤除这一抖动——VCO 输出从不跟随它。把分频器沿时序当"输出相位"记录，jitter 虚高 600 倍。修正：以 ∫(fv−fout)dt + 振荡器噪声累加真实输出相位。

9.2 Sign correlators go blind at a biased equilibrium9.2 平衡点偏置使符号相关器失明

CP mismatch + leakage shift the locked PD error to ≈ −1.5 ps, so sign(Δt) = −1 essentially always and sign-sign LMS stops discriminating. Standard hardware practice — remove the error DC with an EMA before taking the sign — is now built into every correlator in calibration/lms.py. CP 失配 + 泄漏把锁定点鉴相误差偏移到约 −1.5 ps，sign(Δt) 几乎恒为 −1，符号-符号 LMS 失去鉴别力。硬件的标准做法——取符号前先用 EMA 去除误差直流——现已内建于 calibration/lms.py 的所有相关器。

9.3 DTC range must cover the MASH residue span9.3 DTC 量程必须覆盖 MASH 残差跨度

MASH-3 residue spans ~8 UI = 1.33 ns at 6 GHz — far beyond a 512 ps DTC. The DTC saturated, cancellation failed at the extremes, and the LUT calibration chased the saturation error divergently. Real DTC-based synthesizers choose MASH 1-1 (or EFM1) for exactly this reason; so does ex02. MASH-3 残差跨度约 8 UI，即 6 GHz 下 1.33 ns——远超 512 ps 的 DTC 量程。DTC 饱和，两端对消失效，LUT 校准追逐饱和误差而发散。真实 DTC 型频综正是因此选择 MASH 1-1（或 EFM1）；ex02 亦然。

9.4 Closed-loop two-point KDCO measurement carries no information9.4 闭环两点 KDCO 测量不含信息

The loop integrator absorbs an OTW perturbation exactly, so the steady state is independent of KDCO — the estimator collapsed to zero and the loop diverged on the division. FCAL must run open-loop (as real ADPLLs sequence it), then close the loop. 环路积分器把 OTW 扰动精确吸收，稳态与 KDCO 无关——估计值坍缩到零，除法发散。FCAL 必须开环进行（真实 ADPLL 正是如此排序），然后再闭环。

9.5 An acquisition aid can fight the loop it serves9.5 捕获辅助环路可能与主环打架

A hard bang-bang FLL kept pushing full current during its hysteresis wait, drove the frequency back out of the SSPD capture range, and the pair limit-cycled at 150 kHz forever. A dead zone instead handed off parked at the band edge — also bad. The working answer: bang-bang outside the release band, proportional taper inside. 硬 bang-bang FLL 在迟滞等待期持续满电流输出，把频率重新推出 SSPD 捕获范围，两个环路以 150 kHz 永久极限环振荡。改成死区则停在带边沿交接——同样不行。可行答案：释放带外 bang-bang，带内比例衰减。

9.6 A linear injection-locking model never loses lock9.6 线性注入锁定模型永不失锁

The realignment recursion is linear and converges for any frequency error — physically absurd. The missing piece is the nearest-edge ±π wrap: with it, the model slips beyond |Δf| = βfref/2, matching the classical result, and the simulator counts slips explicitly. 重排递推是线性的，对任意频差都收敛——物理上荒谬。缺的是"最近沿 ±π 卷绕"：加上之后，模型在 |Δf| = βfref/2 之外失锁，与经典结果一致，仿真器并显式计数滑移。

Honest deviations from the original plan. InjTimingCal is provided but not wired into the ex06 main flow. 与原计划的诚实偏差。原计划对 DTC 增益校准断言"杂散改善 >15 dB"，后证明指标选错了：未校准增益误差泄漏的是宽带整形噪声而非窄带杂散。实际测试判据改为 jitter 恢复（5 ps → 528 fs → 167 fs，相位功率约 30 dB），INL 部分为 jitter 降低 >20%、杂散改善约 8 dB（受 LUT bin 分辨率限制）。InjTimingCal 已提供但未接入 ex06 主流程。

11.5 Engine speed and Monte Carlo yield analysis (ex11)11.5 引擎提速与 Monte Carlo 良率分析（ex11）

Profiling showed 78% of simulation time in two scipy.linalg.expm calls per reference cycle (variable pulse widths defeated caching). 11.6 Multiplying DLL (ex12)11.6 倍频 DLL（ex12）

The ILCM's β→1 limit: once per Tref the oscillator edge is replaced by the clean reference edge, so accumulated jitter resets and the oscillator noise sees NTF = 1−ZOH (|NTF| ≈ πf/fref at low offset). A bang-bang digital tuning loop keeps the ring period aligned; its residual drift is the fref spur, same sawtooth analytic as the ILCM. The honest three-way result on the same −95 dBc/Hz ring: MDLL 281 fs ≈ strong-injection ILCM (β=0.6, 290 fs), but weak injection collapses (β=0.2: 891 fs) — edge replacement is insurance against injection strength, not magic; and its reference/mux noise runs flat to Nyquist, which costs when the ring is quiet. 11.8 Deterministic fractional-spur prediction (ex15)11.8 确定性小数杂散预测（ex15）

Fractional spurs are deterministic, not statistical: the MASH residual is periodic in the accumulator, so DTC code quantization, INL and residual gain error become coherent tones at fold(k·frac)·fref. core.dtcspurs replays the bit-true code sequence through the DTC transfer, projects it onto the exact tone frequencies (Hann-windowed DTFT — no bin leakage) and refers each tone through the loop's DTC noise transfer; all four fractional analyze() paths publish the table in spurs_analytic. Validation: 500 fs sine INL on the Wu'19-class SPLL — prediction vs noise-free time domain agrees within 0.2 dB at all four tones. The worst-channel law falls out: near-integer channels beat inside the loop BW where |NTF| ~ 1, and Wu's <−64 dBc class corresponds to 30–50 fs of post-linearization INL — the real spec hidden behind "a highly linear DTC". Also documented: measured-spur readings are floor-limited once tones sink below the local noise, and the live gain LMS drags in-band INL tones down a few dB by tracking the beat. 小数杂散是确定性的而非统计的：MASH 残差在累加器意义下周期，DTC 码量化、INL 与残余增益误差都变成 fold(k·frac)·fref 处的相干音。core.dtcspurs 把位真码序列重放过 DTC 传递，在精确音频点上投影（Hann 加窗 DTFT——无栅栏泄漏），再经环路 DTC 噪声传递折算到输出；四条小数 analyze() 路径都在 spurs_analytic 里发布该表。验证：Wu'19 级 SPLL 加 500 fs 正弦 INL——预测与无噪声时域四个音全部吻合在 0.2 dB 内。最差通道规律自然浮现：近整数通道的差拍落在环路带宽内（|NTF| ~ 1），Wu 的 <−64 dBc 档对应 30–50 fs 的线性化后 INL——"高线性 DTC"背后的真实指标。同时如实记录：音强低于本底后实测读数受噪底限制，且在线增益 LMS 会跟踪带内差拍、把 INL 音再压低几 dB。

11.10 Two-point modulation and EVM (ex17)11.10 两点调制与 EVM（ex17）

11.12 Channel-hop settling and the FLL hand-off bound (ex19)11.12 跳频建立时间与 FLL 交接边界（ex19）

pllsim.settling measures a retune as acquisition from the previous channel's operating point and extracts frequency pull-in (counter-style detector, tolerance floored at the loop's own settled wander) and phase settling (last excursion beyond 50 mrad), plus the FLL segment. The ADPLL retunes in ~9 µs — no FLL slew segment; sampling loops are FLL-dominated at hundreds of µs, linear in hop size (a charge budget: ΔV = hop/Kvco on C1 at ifll). The analysis surfaced a design rule now enforced as fll_stability(): the bang-bang FLL decides once per measurement window, so beyond ifll,max = frelease·fref·C1/(2·Kvco·W) its limit-cycle amplitude exceeds the release band and it oscillates around zero forever without handing off — more current is NOT faster. Three presets sat on the bound and now carry 1.4–1.9× margin. A 20-seed population shows settling is a yield quantity: spec the p95, not the mean. pllsim.settling 把重调谐当作"从上一通道工作点起捕获"来测量：频率牵引（计数器式检测，容差以环路自身稳态频率抖动为底）与相位建立（最后一次超出 50 mrad），并给出 FLL 段时长。ADPLL 约 9 µs 完成重调——没有 FLL 段；采样类环路被 FLL 段主导在数百 µs，且与跳频量线性（电荷预算：C1 上 ΔV = hop/Kvco，电流 ifll）。分析暴露出一条设计规则并固化为 fll_stability()：bang-bang FLL 每测量窗口只判决一次，超过 ifll,max = frelease·fref·C1/(2·Kvco·W) 后极限环幅度超出释放带，围绕零点永远振荡、永不交接给鉴相器——电流更大不等于更快。三个 preset 原本压在边界上，现已留 1.4–1.9× 裕量。20 种子分布说明建立时间是良率量：按 p95 而不是均值定指标。

11.13 Background calibration under drift (ex20)11.13 漂移下的后台校准（ex20）

The fractional engines accept a per-cycle true-gain trajectory (dtc_gain_drift) — an accelerated temperature ramp. Two stacked mechanisms set the tracking lag: the EMA error-centering (mandatory for static offsets) blinds the correlator during ramps (~1% lag even well below the slew wall), and the sign-sign slew limit (max mu per cycle) adds beyond rate ~ mu. Every % of lag is a live in-band fractional spur at 20log10(lag). The mu window has two walls — worst drift rate below, gradient-noise wander above (measured: 0.005% → 0.087% rms gain wander from mu 5e-7 → 5e-5). Scaled back to silicon, a 600 ppm/°C DTC on a 10 °C/s transient drifts 5 orders below the wall: temperature is not the threat — step events (band switch, power bursts) are, and those are ex19's settling problem. 小数引擎接受逐周期真实增益轨迹（dtc_gain_drift）——加速温度斜坡。跟踪滞后由两个机制叠加决定：EMA 误差去直流（静态失调所必需）在斜坡期间使相关器部分失明（即使远低于转换率墙也有 ~1% 滞后），符号-符号转换率极限（每周期最多 mu）在 rate ~ mu 以上叠加。每 1% 滞后都是实打实的带内小数杂散（20log10(lag)）。mu 窗口有两面墙——下限是最坏漂移速率，上限是梯度噪声游走（实测：mu 从 5e-7 到 5e-5，增益游走 0.005% → 0.087% rms）。换算回硅片：600 ppm/°C 的 DTC 在 10 °C/s 热瞬变下漂移速率比墙低 5 个数量级——温度不是威胁，阶跃事件（换频段、突发功率）才是，而那是 ex19 建立时间分析的领域。

11.15 Oscillator control-voltage range and the coarse band bank11.15 振荡器控制电压范围与粗调频段组

Configuring n_bands and band_step_hz used to change nothing measurable, and the reason was that nothing limited the control voltage: one band reached any frequency, so the binary band search always succeeded on whatever band it started in. OscConfig now carries v_min/v_max (default None = the old unlimited behaviour) and freq_law clamps to them, so a band that cannot reach the target rails instead of pretending to. That makes a sizing constraint real: a band spans 2·gain·v_max while the bank steps by band_step_hz, so the bands only join up when the span exceeds the step. band_overlap_hz() reports the margin and goes negative for a gap, band_bank_is_continuous() and bank_range_hz() answer the two questions that follow, and both GUIs show the four numbers above the workbench form — a bank with a hole otherwise reads as a loop that will not lock. v_for is deliberately left unclamped so a caller can see that a target needs a voltage the varactor does not have. 以前配 n_bands 和 band_step_hz 不产生任何可测量的影响，原因是没有任何东西限制控制电压：一个 band 就能到达任意频率，于是二分频段搜索总在起始 band 上就"成功"了。OscConfig 现在带 v_min/v_max（默认 None＝原来的不限行为），freq_law 对其钳位，够不到目标的 band 会撞轨而不是假装能到。这让一条尺寸约束变成真的：单个 band 跨度约 2·gain·v_max，而频段组以 band_step_hz 步进，只有跨度超过步进时相邻 band 才接得上。band_overlap_hz() 给出裕量（负值＝有空洞），band_bank_is_continuous() 与 bank_range_hz() 回答随之而来的两个问题，两个 GUI 都把这四个数显示在工作台表单上方——否则一个有空洞的频段组读起来就像"环路锁不上"。v_for 故意不钳位，好让调用者看出目标需要的电压超出了变容管范围。

11.17 What a completeness audit found (v0.6.0)11.17 完备性审计发现了什么（v0.6.0）

Auditing the project against its own claims turned up three corrections where analyze() and simulate() modelled the same effect differently. Sampler gm noise was 3 dB low: the gm stage injects one charge packet per reference cycle, which is a sampled sequence carrying the 2× alias factor into the one-sided PSD (the same factor SampledKTC always had), but it was modelled as a duty-cycled continuous current. The charge pump had it right — by folding the 2 into its duty argument, so nothing in the code said which convention was which; there is now an explicit SampledChargeNoise. The reference spur was 6 dB optimistic in all three analog architectures: narrowband FM puts the sideband at β/2 with β = Kvco·V1/fref, and the code carried an extra factor of 2 inside β and halved again. Nothing caught it because the reference spur cannot be observed in the time domain at all — the engines step once per reference cycle, so a tone at fref folds to DC. DTC random jitter never reached the CPPLL or ADPLL budgets although every fractional engine injects it each cycle; in the bang-bang ADPLL it was missing from the comparator's σ_t as well, so it moved neither the predicted noise nor the predicted bandwidth. 把工程对照它自己的说法审计一遍，查出三处 analyze() 与 simulate() 对同一效应建模不一致。采样器 gm 噪声低 3 dB：gm 级每参考周期注入一个电荷包，这是采样序列，单边 PSD 要带 2× 混叠因子（SampledKTC 一直带着），却被建成占空比降低的连续电流源。电荷泵是对的——但它把这个 2 折进了 duty 参数里，代码里没有任何地方说明这是两种约定；现在有了显式的 SampledChargeNoise。参考杂散在三个模拟架构上乐观了 6 dB：窄带 FM 边带是 β/2、β = Kvco·V1/fref，而代码在 β 里多带了一个 2 又除了一次。没人发现是因为参考杂散在时域根本测不到——引擎每参考周期只走一步，fref 处的音直接折叠到 DC。DTC 随机抖动从未进入 CPPLL 与 ADPLL 的预算，尽管每个小数引擎每周期都注入它；在 bang-bang ADPLL 里它连比较器的 σ_t 都没进，于是既不改预测噪声也不改预测带宽。

The second class was inputs accepted and then ignored. ADPLL.simulate() advertised six knobs on one signature but handed each mode only its own subset — a bang-bang two-point modulation run returned a perfectly normal-looking result with no modulation in it, and the EVM read as noise-limited. OscConfig's tuning nonlinearity, supply pushing and band bank reached only some engines. fll_stability() raised AttributeError on architectures with no FLL instead of saying the question does not apply. dtc_lut_cal was consumed by the CPPLL alone. The rule applied throughout: implement it, or refuse it with the reason — never accept and drop. Where refusing won, it is because wiring was tried and measured: the INL LUT made the spur worse in the other three architectures with either update sign, because it regresses the detector's timing error against the MASH residue and those detectors expose a different quantity. 第二类是"接受了却不起作用"的输入。ADPLL.simulate() 在一个签名上公开六个旋钮，却只把各自的子集传给对应模式——bang-bang 的两点调制运行会返回一份完全正常的结果，里面没有任何调制，EVM 读起来是噪声受限。OscConfig 的调谐非线性、供电推频与频段组只到达了部分引擎。fll_stability() 对没有 FLL 的架构抛 AttributeError，而不是说这个问题不适用。dtc_lut_cal 只有 CPPLL 消费。全程遵循的规则是：要么实现，要么带原因拒绝——绝不接受后丢弃。凡是选择拒绝的地方，都是因为先接线再实测过：INL LUT 在另外三个架构上无论更新符号取正负都让杂散更差，因为它把鉴相器的时间误差对 MASH 残差做回归，而那些鉴相器暴露的是另一个量。

Why the existing tests could not see any of it: the cross-domain regressions compare totals with 2.5–3 dB tolerances, which is exactly wide enough to hide a 3 dB error in one contributor. tests/test_conventions.py takes the opposite approach — drive one source to >90 % of the budget, then compare — and that is what pins the sampled-injection factor, the narrowband-FM sideband and the DTC-jitter path. 为什么现有测试一个都没看见：跨域回归比的是总量、容差 2.5–3 dB，正好宽到能藏住单项 3 dB 的错。tests/test_conventions.py 反过来做——把一个源抬到占预算 >90 % 再比——采样注入因子、窄带 FM 边带与 DTC 抖动路径就是这样钉住的。

11.18 The reference spur, made observable and made right (v0.7.0)11.18 参考杂散：先让它可观测，再把它改对（v0.7.0）

§11.17 closes by noting that the reference spur "cannot be observed in the time domain at all". That was true of the engines as they stood, and accepting it was the mistake: a convention no test can reach is a convention that drifts. simulate(fine_oversample=M) now records M control-voltage samples per reference period on CPPLL/SSPLL/SPLL. At M=1 — still the default, so nothing changes unasked — the ripple sits exactly at the record's own sampling rate and aliases to DC, which is precisely why analyze() could predict a spur nothing could contradict. Making it checkable found the expression still wrong, by 38 dB, in the other direction. §11.17 结尾说参考杂散"在时域根本测不到"。就当时的引擎而言这是事实，但接受它是个错误：测不到的约定就是会漂的约定。现在 simulate(fine_oversample=M) 让 CPPLL/SSPLL/SPLL 每参考周期记录 M 个控制电压样点。M=1（仍是默认，不主动改变任何已有结果）时纹波正好落在记录自身的采样率上、混叠到直流——这正是 analyze() 能预测一个无从反驳的杂散的原因。一旦可检验，就发现那个式子仍然是错的，差 38 dB，而且方向相反。

The model treated the per-period impairment charge as a single impulse of area dq, giving a fundamental of peak amplitude 2·dq·fref. That is right for leakage and wrong for mismatch, and the difference is the loop itself. A type-II loop has infinite DC gain, so in lock it has already driven the net per-period charge to zero — it sits at whatever static timing offset makes that true. What remains is not one impulse but two of opposite sign: one source conducts alone for |dt|, then both conduct for t_reset. They cancel in area, and their fref component survives only through their separation in time, a factor 2·sin(π·fref·Δt) below the single-impulse answer — 38 dB for a 200 ps reset pulse at 19.2 MHz. Leakage escapes this because a constant current has no fref component at all: the whole fundamental comes from the narrow correction pulse, one impulse, and the old expression was exactly right. Both now fall out of one segment-level Fourier sum with no special-casing. Analytic and time domain agree within 1 dB, converging to 0.01 dB once M resolves the reset pulse. 模型把每周期的非理想电荷当成一个面积 dq 的冲激，基波峰值 2·dq·fref。这对泄漏成立、对失配不成立，差别来自环路本身。二型环路直流增益无穷，所以锁定时它早已把每周期净电荷拉到零——它停在使之成立的那个静态时间偏移上。剩下的不是一个冲激，而是一对反号脉冲：先单边导通 |dt|，再双边导通 t_reset。二者面积抵消，fref 分量只能靠时间上的间隔存活，比单冲激答案低 2·sin(π·fref·Δt) 倍——19.2 MHz、200 ps 复位脉冲下就是 38 dB。泄漏能躲开这一点，因为恒定电流根本没有 fref 分量：整个基波都来自那个窄补偿脉冲，就是一个冲激，旧式子完全正确。现在两者都从同一个分段傅里叶和里出来，不做特例。解析与时域吻合在 1 dB 内，M 足够分辨复位脉冲后收敛到 0.01 dB。

The sub-sampling architectures stopped reporting a reference spur altogether, and that is a result rather than a regression. Their spur was computed from the sampling pedestal — but the pedestal adds to the held voltage, and the gm converts that same held voltage over that same window, so the loop parks where A·sin(perr) + pedestal = 0 and the gm then delivers no charge at all. No charge is no ripple: a static pedestal produces a static phase offset and nothing else. That is a real property of the architecture and much of why sub-sampling loops are known for clean reference spurs; the oversampled record confirms it at −276 dBc. The mechanism that does survive is the sampling clock's kickback, which lands at a different instant from the pulse cancelling it — new SamplerConfig.kick_q_c / kick_delay_s. The presets ship without one and say so in notes: inventing a kickback charge to keep a number on the page would be fabricating the measurement. 子采样架构干脆不再报参考杂散，这是结论而不是退化。它们的杂散原本从采样踏步电压算出——但踏步加在保持电压上，而 gm 又在同一个窗口把这个保持电压转成电荷，于是环路停在 A·sin(perr) + pedestal = 0 处，gm 随后一点电荷都不送。没有电荷就没有纹波：静态踏步只产生静态相位偏移，此外什么都没有。这是该架构的真实性质，也是子采样环路以参考杂散干净著称的主要原因；过采样记录在 −276 dBc 上证实了这一点。真正存活下来的机制是采样时钟的 kickback，它落在与抵消它的脉冲不同的时刻——新增 SamplerConfig.kick_q_c / kick_delay_s。预设不带 kickback，并在 notes 里说明：为了让页面上留个数字而编一个 kickback 电荷，那是在捏造测量结果。

11.19 Impairments that were absent rather than wrong (v0.8.0)11.19 缺失而非错误的非理想效应（v0.8.0）

Four additions, all defaulting off, so no published number moves. TDC INL is now predicted rather than left to simulate(): with a fractional FCW the TDC input sweeps its range at the beat rate, so the normalized code is (n·frac) mod 1 and a declared INL is a fixed function sampled along that ramp — expand it in a Fourier series on [0,1) and harmonic k lands at fold(k·frac)·fref. BBPD metastability has a closed form worth knowing: inside the flop's resolution window the decision is a coin flip, but it still emits ±1, so output power is unchanged and the entire effect lands on the gain — Kbb(W)/Kbb(0) = exp(−W²/2σ²), i.e. 4.34 dB for a window equal to σ. Injection pulling couples into the tank, not the loop, which is why it reaches all six architectures and why chasing it in the loop filter never works; an aggressor inside the lock range reports no sideband and says the oscillator is captured instead. Named PVT corners answer the question Monte Carlo does not: on cppll_19p2m_4p8g the bandwidth spans 2.2× across the standard box. The corner carries an oscillator phase-noise axis because without it the injection-locked multipliers are byte-identical at every corner — their bandwidth is fref/π by construction — which reads as immunity rather than as an unmodelled axis. 四项补充，全部默认关闭，因此没有任何已发布数字变动。TDC INL 现在可以预测而不必交给 simulate()：小数 FCW 让 TDC 输入按拍频扫过量程，归一化码就是 (n·frac) mod 1，声明的 INL 是沿这条斜坡采样的固定函数——在 [0,1) 上做傅里叶展开，第 k 次谐波落在 fold(k·frac)·fref。BBPD 亚稳态有一个值得记住的闭式解：在触发器分辨窗内决策是抛硬币，但它仍然输出 ±1，所以输出功率不变、全部影响落在增益上——Kbb(W)/Kbb(0) = exp(−W²/2σ²)，窗口等于 σ 时是 4.34 dB。注入牵引耦合进的是谐振腔而不是环路，这既是它必须覆盖全部六个架构的原因，也是在环路滤波器里追它从来没用的原因；干扰源落在锁定范围内时不报边带，而是说明振荡器已被俘获。具名 PVT 工艺角回答蒙特卡洛回答不了的问题：cppll_19p2m_4p8g 的带宽在标准角箱上跨 2.2×。工艺角带一条振荡器相噪轴，因为没有它，注入锁定倍频器在每个角上逐位相同——它们的带宽按构造就是 fref/π——那看起来像"免疫"，其实是"没建模"。

11.21 Decorative parameters, an unverified 4.3 dB, and documentation as code (v0.9.1)11.21 装饰性参数、没验证过的 4.3 dB，以及把文档当代码（v0.9.1）

Every standard corner names a supply — SS_125C_0.9V — and nothing in the model ever read it: sweeping Corner.vdd from 0.5 to 2.0 moved the jitter by exactly zero. It now acts through the oscillator's own pushing_hz_v, which is the only honest route, since an oscillator states Hz/V and a corner states a relative supply, so the two need a nominal in volts to meet. A preset that has not characterised pushing comes back untouched rather than having a sensitivity invented for it. In the same file apply_corner was rebuilding only the sub-configs its corner happened to scale, so at TT — which scales nothing, and which corner_report runs first — the returned PLL shared its whole config with the nominal; anything downstream that edited it corrupted the baseline for every later corner. 每一个标准工艺角都写了电源电压——SS_125C_0.9V——而模型里从来没有任何地方读过它：把 Corner.vdd 从 0.5 扫到 2.0，抖动一点都不变。现在它经由振荡器自身的 pushing_hz_v 起作用，这是唯一诚实的路径：振荡器给的是 Hz/V，工艺角给的是相对电源，两者要相遇就需要一个以伏特为单位的标称值。没有标定过推频的 preset 会原样返回，而不是被凭空安上一个灵敏度。同一个文件里，apply_corner 只重建了该工艺角恰好缩放到的那些子配置，于是在 TT（它什么都不缩放，而且 corner_report 第一个就跑它）返回的 PLL 与标称共享整份配置；下游任何对它的修改都会污染后面每一个工艺角的基线。

The TDC-INL spur predictor read 4.3 dB pessimistic against its own time domain, and nothing compared the two. The cause was which variable the INL is a function of: TDCConfig declares the shape over the code range, but the loop only ever walks one oscillator period of it, and a TDC is built with range > Tosc on purpose — here 78% of the axis. Three whole cycles across the range are therefore 2.34 cycles to the loop, which is discontinuous at the wrap, and the wrap is what radiates: the declared line drops and the energy reappears at every other k. With the span correction the dominant spur lands within 0.3 dB of the measurement and the harmonics the model previously did not predict at all show up where they are measured. TDC INL 杂散预测比它自家的时域结果悲观 4.3 dB，而从来没有东西去对比这两者。原因在于 INL 到底是谁的函数：TDCConfig 声明的形状是沿码范围的，而环路只走过其中一个振荡周期，且 TDC 本就是刻意做成量程 > Tosc 的——这里只用到 78% 的轴。于是"整个范围上三个整周期"对环路而言是 2.34 个周期，在回绕处不连续，而辐射出来的正是这个回绕：声明的那根谱线掉下去，能量出现在其余每一个 k 上。加上 span 修正后，主杂散与实测相差 0.3 dB，此前模型完全预测不出的各次谐波也出现在实测的位置上。

Intra-period sampling is what makes the reference spur visible at all, and it was stepping the loop filter M times per reference cycle in Python — 73% of a spur run's time, which is why M was quietly kept small. The system is LTI and diagonal in its eigenbasis, so every sample time has a closed form; it is now evaluated for all M at once and is 7.8× faster at M = 512, bit-for-bit identical. The one subtlety is that the exponents must be of clipped elapsed time: the algebraically equivalent ewt(e−wa − e−wb)/w overflows for a fast pole, because e−w·tstep is the reciprocal of a number the stable dynamics have already made tiny. Both GUIs now expose M, with the two things that go wrong printed next to it: a sub-interval coarser than the charge pump's reset pulse reads the spur low, and the record is M floats per reference cycle — 600 MB at 150k cycles and M = 512. 周期内细采样是参考杂散能被看见的唯一原因，而它此前是在 Python 里每个参考周期把环路滤波器推进 M 次——占一次杂散运行 73% 的时间，这也是 M 一直被悄悄压小的原因。这个系统是线性时不变的、在特征基下对角，所以每个采样时刻都有闭式解；现在一次性对全部 M 个时刻求值，M = 512 时快 7.8×，逐位相同。唯一的微妙之处是指数必须是钳位后的已过时间：代数上等价的 ewt(e−wa − e−wb)/w 对快极点会溢出，因为 e−w·tstep 正是稳定动力学已经压到极小的那个数的倒数。两个 GUI 现在都暴露了 M，并把两件会出错的事印在它旁边：子区间比电荷泵复位脉冲还粗时杂散会读低，而记录量是每参考周期 M 个浮点数——150k 周期、M = 512 就是 600 MB。
