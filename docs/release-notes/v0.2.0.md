pllsim v0.2.0 — literature anchors, spur prediction, bench-data fitting,
two-point modulation, architecture selection

Highlights since v0.1.0:

- SPLL fractional-N mode: EFM1 + DTC delaying the divided edge (constant
  reference sampling phase) with sign-sign LMS background gain calibration;
  wired through the full Verilog-AMS export bridge (10th exported kind,
  RTL testbenches bit-true under iverilog) and a Wu'19-style preset
- Four-paper JSSC benchmark anchor (ex14): Dartizio'23 DTC+BBPD digital
  PLL (model 77 fs time-domain vs 77 published — the BBPD nonlinear
  penalty only the time domain captures), Markulic'16 fractional DTC-SSPLL
  (165/199 fs linear vs 176/198 published; near-free fractionalization
  reproduced), Wu'19 fractional sampling PLL (77/78 fs vs 75 published
  over the paper's 10 kHz–10 MHz band), joining Gao'09 (ex10)
- Deterministic fractional-spur prediction (core/dtcspurs, ex15): bit-true
  MASH residuals through the DTC transfer (quantization + INL + gain
  residual), exact-frequency DTFT projection, loop-NTF referral; published
  in every fractional analyze() spurs_analytic table; 0.2 dB against the
  noise-free time domain; worst-channel law and INL-spec extraction
- Bench-data import and fitting (pllsim.fit, ex16): E5052-style CSV
  loader; Leeson triple fit (0.2 dB / 4% corner from 0.5 dB-noisy data);
  direct closed-loop estimators (plateau, corner, peaking); NNLS budget
  attribution with shape-identifiability groups — names the over-budget
  source group from a measured curve at 0.4 dB rms residual
- Two-point GMSK modulation + EVM (pllsim.modulation, ex17): FCW+DCO
  (ADPLL) and DTC+VCO (SSPLL) injection; matched EVM -43.5 dB at 10 Mb/s
  through a 1 MHz loop; EVM-vs-mismatch design curves; SSPLL lands in
  Markulic'16's -40 dB class; engine discretization floor documented
- Architecture selector (pllsim.selector, ex18): all seven architectures
  instantiated from technology-class templates, loops synthesized, ranked
  against a Requirement with hard infeasibilities excluded and explained;
  recommends the sampling PLL for the Wu'19-like plan — the architecture
  the actual paper chose
- Suite at 124 tests; docs sections 11.8–11.11; examples ex14–ex18
