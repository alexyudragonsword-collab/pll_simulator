pllsim v0.3.0 — channel-hop settling and drift-tracking validation

Highlights since v0.2.0:

- Channel-hop settling analysis (pllsim.settling, ex19): a retune is
  measured as acquisition from the previous channel's operating point;
  frequency pull-in uses a counter-style detector whose tolerance floors
  at the loop's own settled wander, phase settling is the last excursion
  beyond 50 mrad; FLL segment reported separately; hop_statistics() runs
  seed populations (settling is a yield quantity — spec the p95)
- FLL hand-off stability bound (fll_stability()): the bang-bang FLL
  decides once per measurement window, so beyond
  i_fll_max = f_release*fref*C1/(2*Kvco*window) its limit cycle exceeds
  the release band and it never hands off to the phase detector — more
  current is NOT faster.  Three presets sat on the bound and now carry
  1.4-1.9x margin
- Architecture comparison: the ADPLL retunes in ~9 us (no FLL slew
  segment); sampling loops are FLL-dominated at hundreds of us, linear
  in hop size (charge budget on C1)
- Drift-tracking validation (ex20): fractional engines accept a
  per-cycle true DTC-gain trajectory (dtc_gain_drift) — an accelerated
  temperature ramp through the live background LMS.  Two stacked lag
  mechanisms quantified (EMA error-centering blinding during ramps; the
  sign-sign slew limit at rate ~ mu), spur penalty 20log10(lag), the
  two-walled mu_final selection window, and the honest scale-back:
  real thermal ramps sit ~5 orders below the wall — step events are
  the threat, and they belong to the settling analysis
- main branch established; suite at 131 tests; docs 11.12-11.13
