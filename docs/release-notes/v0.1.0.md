pllsim v0.1.0 — system-level / behavioral PLL simulation

First release. Highlights:

- 7 PLL architectures with dual-domain models (linear phase-domain `analyze()`
  + reference-edge-driven behavioral `simulate()`): charge-pump PLL (integer-N
  and fractional-N with MASH+DTC), sub-sampling PLL (integer & DTC-assisted
  fractional, exact discrete loop model), reference-sampling PLL, ADPLL
  (counter+TDC and DTC+BBPD), injection-locked clock multiplier, MDLL
- Noise budgeting: per-source phase-noise breakdown, IPN/RMS-jitter
  integration; time-domain periodograms match the linear models within
  2–3 dB band-averaged (test-enforced); coverage fref 19.2–250 MHz,
  fout to 12 GHz, 50–200 fs jitter class
- Spur analysis: reference / fractional / injection spurs, analytic + FFT
- Calibration: DTC gain (LMS/sign-sign) and INL LUT, KDCO open-loop FCAL,
  TDC period normalization, BBPD linearization, SSPLL FLL, ILCM FTL and
  injection-timing calibration, VCO band selection
- Loop synthesis (filter/DLF values from UGB/PM targets) and
  jitter-vs-bandwidth optimization
- Second-order impairments: Kvco nonlinearity, supply pushing,
  reference-doubler duty error, multi-band coarse tuning
- Monte Carlo yield framework (multiprocess, per-chip calibration)
- Literature benchmark: reproduces Gao et al. JSSC'09/'10 sub-sampling PLL
  published measurements (−126 dBc/Hz in-band, 0.15 ps class)
- Verilog-AMS export bridge (Cadence): bit-true synthesizable RTL for the
  digital blocks (iverilog-verified at zero tolerance), cycle-true
  wreal/RNM tops with golden-CSV testbenches, electrical VAMS netlists
- 100 pytest tests, 13 runnable examples, bilingual (EN/中文) illustrated
  design guide under docs/
